# 📊 Project 4 – Data Visualization | DecodeLabs

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of the **DecodeLabs Data Analytics Industrial Training Program –** under Project 4: Data Visualization&#x20;

The objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights that support business understanding and data-driven decision-making.

The project focuses on selecting appropriate charts, identifying business trends, highlighting key findings, and communicating analytical results through effective data storytelling.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze e-commerce sales and order data.
- Create meaningful and easy-to-understand visualizations.
- Identify product-wise and year-wise sales trends.
- Analyze customer payment preferences and referral sources.
- Evaluate order status and coupon code performance.
- Extract actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Overall Performance:** The dataset recorded total sales of ₹12,64,761.96 across 1,200 orders, with an Average Order Value of ₹1,053.97.

- **Product Performance:** Chair generated the highest revenue of ₹1,95,620.11, closely followed by Printer.

- **Sales Trend:** 2023 recorded the highest annual revenue of ₹5,52,643.24. Revenue decreased in 2024 compared with 2023.

- **Order Status:** Cancelled orders accounted for 250 transactions, making it the most frequent order status.

- **Payment Preferences:** Online payments recorded the highest number of orders (258).

- **Marketing Performance:** Instagram generated the highest number of orders (259) and the highest revenue of ₹2,75,285.45.

- **Coupon Performance:** FREESHIP was the most frequently used coupon code, appearing in 313 orders.

- **Quantity Relationship:** Quantity and Total Price showed a moderately positive correlation of approximately 0.615.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing products to strengthen sales performance.
2. Investigate the reasons behind declining annual revenue.
3. Analyze cancellation and return patterns to improve order fulfilment.
4. Strengthen marketing campaigns through high-performing referral channels.
5. Evaluate coupon code effectiveness and promotional strategies.
6. Introduce product bundles and quantity-based offers to improve average order value.
